Predictive Retail Revenue Engine

Applied Machine Learning & Operational Intelligence

1. Executive Summary & Business Problem

Designed and prototyped an automated demand and foot-traffic forecasting model to help retail operations managers optimize weekly inventory replenishment and staff scheduling, directly tying machine learning predictions to dollar variances and operational efficiency.

2. Technical Architecture & Execution Stack

  • Language & Environment: Python, NumPy, Pandas
  • Machine Learning Libraries: scikit-learn (`LinearRegression`, `train_test_split`, `mean_absolute_error`, `r2_score`)
  • Validation Framework: Out-of-sample holdout testing with controlled random seeding to simulate unseen operational environments.

3. Methodology & Rigorous Validation

Established a naive baseline Mean Absolute Error (MAE) benchmark ($5,329) before deploying a multivariate linear regression pipeline ($y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x_1 + \dots + \beta_n x_n$). The final optimized model achieved a test MAE of ~$2,800 and an $R^2$ of 0.72 on unseen operational holdout data.

4. Risk Mitigation & Governance

Data Leakage Prevention: Systematically audited feature spaces to purge target-derived variables (such as direct sales tax collections) that would invalidate real-world Monday-morning deployment. Established strict extrapolation boundaries to prevent out-of-bounds predictions on historical outliers.

Technical Core & Methodology

Execution Engine (Python)
